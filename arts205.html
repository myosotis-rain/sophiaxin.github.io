Overview

Animated portfolio created for meditated drawing course, featuring interactive presentations and dynamic visual elements that explore the intersection of traditional drawing techniques with digital animation.

This academic portfolio documents a semester-long journey through contemplative drawing practices, translating traditional meditative art forms into engaging digital presentations that maintain the reflective essence of the original coursework.

Key Features

  • Animated Presentations

    Custom slideshow animations with contemplative transitions that guide viewers through the artistic journey at a meditative pace.

  • Interactive Elements

    Visual elements that respond to user engagement while maintaining the contemplative nature of the original drawing practices.

  • Digital-Traditional Integration

    Seamless blending of traditional drawing documentation with modern digital enhancement techniques.

  • Narrative Structure

    Thoughtfully organized presentation flow that mirrors the reflective process of meditative drawing practice.

Creative Approach

The challenge was to translate meditative drawing practices into engaging digital animations while maintaining the contemplative essence:

Contemplative Pacing

Animation timing designed to honor the slow, meditative process of the original art practice.

Visual Continuity

Seamless transitions that maintain the spiritual and artistic integrity of the drawing journey.

Academic Integration

Presentation format that serves both artistic expression and academic documentation requirements.

Successfully documented semester-long artistic journey with innovative presentation format that enhanced the viewing experience while preserving the meditative qualities of the original work.
