Work / Vinetality

Vinetality

Tangible Interface for Productivity & Well-being

Role Interface Designer & Developer
Course Tangible User Interfaces (MIT)
Technologies Arduino, 3D Printing, Sensors
Year 2025

Project Overview

Vinetality is a small, portable keychain interface that provides tangible feedback to support productivity, well-being, and community building through a shape-changing plant representation.

The device uses physical form changes to communicate states of wellness and productivity, creating an intimate and personal connection between the user and their digital habits. By representing growth and health through a familiar plant metaphor, Vinetality bridges the gap between abstract digital metrics and tangible, meaningful feedback.

Design Concept

The core concept revolves around using a physical plant-like interface that responds to user behavior and environmental factors. As users engage in productive activities or maintain healthy habits, the "plant" grows and flourishes. Neglect or poor habits cause it to wilt or contract.

This tangible representation creates emotional investment in personal growth while providing immediate, visceral feedback about lifestyle choices and productivity patterns.

Technical Implementation

  • Arduino-based microcontroller for sensor processing and actuator control
  • Shape-memory alloys and servo motors for physical transformation
  • Bluetooth connectivity for smartphone integration
  • Custom 3D-printed enclosure designed for portability
  • Mobile app for habit tracking and device configuration

Impact

Created a novel approach to habit formation that combines emotional design with tangible interaction, demonstrating how physical interfaces can motivate behavioral change.
