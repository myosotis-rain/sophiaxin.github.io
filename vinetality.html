Tangible Interface for Productivity & Well-being

Interface Designer & Developer
Tangible User Interfaces (MIT)

Overview

Vinetality is a small, portable keychain interface that provides tangible feedback to support productivity, well-being, and community building through a shape-changing plant representation.

The device uses physical form changes to communicate states of wellness and productivity, creating an intimate and personal connection between the user and their digital habits. By representing growth and health through a familiar plant metaphor, Vinetality bridges the gap between abstract digital metrics and tangible, meaningful feedback.

Key Features

  • Shape-Changing Feedback

    Physical plant-like interface that responds to user behavior, growing when engaging in productive activities and wilting with neglect.

  • Portable Design

    Compact keychain form factor with custom 3D-printed enclosure designed for everyday portability and durability.

  • Smart Integration

    Bluetooth connectivity with smartphone integration and mobile app for habit tracking and device configuration.

  • Community Building

    Social features that connect users and create accountability networks for productivity and well-being goals.

Technical Approach

The design combines emotional design principles with advanced tangible interaction technologies to create meaningful behavioral feedback:

Physical Computing

Arduino-based microcontroller with shape-memory alloys and servo motors for transformation.

Sensor Integration

Environmental and behavioral sensors for comprehensive habit and wellness tracking.

Emotional Design

Plant metaphor creates emotional investment and intuitive understanding of personal growth.

Technologies

Arduino 3D Printing Interface Design IoT Tangible UI Sensors

Impact

Created a novel approach to habit formation that combines emotional design with tangible interaction, demonstrating how physical interfaces can motivate behavioral change and foster community connections.
